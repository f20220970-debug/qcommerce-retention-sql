# Results summary

- Scale: 206,209 customers, 3,346,083 orders, 49,688 products; 10.11 items per order; 16.23 orders per customer.
- 59.0% of all items bought were reorders.
- Most habitual categories: dairy eggs 67%, beverages 65%, produce 65%; least: international 37%, pantry 35%, personal care 32%.
- Habit window: 51% of repeat orders come within 7 days; peaks at 7 days (10.0%) and at the 30+ cap (11.0%).
- Retention (customers here all have 4+ orders): 52% reach order 10, 26% reach 20, 5% reach 50.
- Concentration: the top 10% of customers place 35% of orders; the top 20% place 53%.
- Width vs retention (correlation): share reaching 10+ orders by departments in the first order: 1: 47%, 2: 50%, 3: 52%, 4: 53%, 5: 53%, 6: 54%, 7+: 54%.
- First basket size vs reaching 10+ orders: 1-5 items: 49%, 6-10 items: 53%, 11-20 items: 54%, 21+ items: 56%.
- Top habit products: Whole Organic Omega 3 Milk (86%), Organic Lactose Free Whole Milk (86%), Milk, Organic, Vitamin D (85%), Organic Reduced Fat Milk (85%), Goat Milk (85%).
- Busiest slot: day 1, 10:00 (54,719 orders).
